# Fill My Form

Relayed leads the visual design; Vercel informs interface discipline. These are references, not brands to reproduce.

- Near-black ink canvas (#171920), white display type, muted lavender text.
- Electric blue to orchid accent, limited to calls to action and a signature flowing line.
- Large centered editorial hero, generous whitespace, compact navigation.
- A bright form preview bridges the dark hero and warm paper sections.
- Use a violet-tinted memory panel beside the form to explain saved-answer reuse.
- White/paper sections for steps and use cases; dark trust and closing sections.
- System sans typography with script-specific system fallbacks. No remote font requests.
- Responsive type, logical spacing, RTL support, natural text wrapping, reduced motion.
- No copied logos, testimonials, company names, or site-specific form data.
- Controls have visible focus, >=44px targets, semantic HTML and contrast-tested text.

References: design-references/relayed/DESIGN.md and design-references/vercel/DESIGN.md.
