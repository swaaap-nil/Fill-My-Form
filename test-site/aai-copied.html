Accepted Photo Guidelines
Please review before uploading your photograph
Photos that will be rejected
Coloured Background
Use plain white/light grey background only
Blurry / Out of Focus
Ensure a sharp, high-resolution image
Cartoon / Avatar
Must be a real photograph, not an illustration
Multiple Faces
Only one person should appear in the frame
No Face Visible
Face must be fully visible and centred
Busy Background
Background must be plain and uncluttered
Photo of a Screen
Do not photograph an image on a display
Head Tilted
Look straight ahead, head level & centred
Poor Lighting
Even, soft lighting with no harsh shadows
Incorrect Framing
Face should fill 70–80% of the frame
Accepted photo format
✓ Accepted

✅ Perfect Photo

  • Plain white / light grey background
  • Face clearly visible, straight gaze
  • Sharp, in-focus image
  • Good, even lighting — no shadows
  • Head level, centred in frame
  • Single person only
How to take the best photo
Natural DaylightFace a window during the day for even, shadow-free light.
Portrait ModeUse your phone's portrait mode to keep your face sharp.
Plain BackgroundStand against a white wall or hang a white sheet.
Solo in FrameEnsure no other person or reflection appears behind you.
Hold SteadyRest your elbow on a surface or use a timer to avoid blur.
Check the CropYour face should fill most of the frame, crown to chin.

Non-compliant photos will be automatically rejected
