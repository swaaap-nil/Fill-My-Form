AI Form Agent — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-05

AI Form Agent is a browser extension that fills web forms from information you give it, stored on your own device. This page explains, in plain language, what that means for your data.

Nothing you type into the extension leaves your device

The personal information you save — names, addresses, phone numbers, and anything else you add — is stored only in your browser's local storage on your computer. The extension does not have a server, and it does not send this information anywhere. There is no account to create and nothing to sync.

The extension only sees pages you specifically allow

When you install the extension, it has no access to any website. The first time you want to use it on a site, your browser will ask you to approve access to that specific site. The extension only runs on sites you've approved this way, one at a time. You can remove this access at any time from your browser's extension settings.

Your data is stored as plain text, protected by your operating system

The information you save is not additionally encrypted by the extension. It is protected the same way any other file on your computer is protected — by your operating system's own file permissions and login. If someone has access to your logged-in computer, they could potentially access this data, the same as they could access any other file on your device.

How to delete everything

Removing the extension deletes all the information it stored. In Chrome or Firefox, you can do this from your browser's extensions page (right-click the extension's icon and choose "Remove", or manage it from the extensions list). No data is left behind on any server, because none was ever sent to one.

Submitting forms

The extension never submits a form on your behalf. It can fill in fields for you to review, but pressing "submit" on any form is always something you do yourself.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
